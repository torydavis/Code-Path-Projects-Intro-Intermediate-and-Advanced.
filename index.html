Price of Our Dogs!!

$1,750 for puppies under 6 months. $1,600 for dogs under 1 year.

It decreases by $100 for each additional full year of age there after.

This donation helps cover rescue care, spay/neuter, and veterinary expenses for this dog and other French Bulldogs in need.

On average, dogs in our rescue cost $1,600+ in medical care, and some require $6,000+ to rehabilitate.

About Us

Independent SoCal French Bulldog Rescue was founded in 2023 by Debra Munt to provide a safe, compassionate path for French Bulldogs in need throughout Southern California. Our rescue is led by a dedicated Board of Directors and is 100% volunteer-run, with volunteers managing every aspect of the organization—from intake, foster coordination, and adoptions to fundraising, transportation, medical support, and behind-the-scenes operations. We rescue dogs from shelters, owner surrenders, and other situations across Southern California, giving each one the medical care, rehabilitation, and love they need before finding their forever home. As a nonprofit rescue, we rely entirely on donations, foster homes, and adopters to continue our mission. Every donation, every foster family, every volunteer, and every adopter plays a vital role in giving these deserving dogs a second chance.
